# 5-0 Rehearsal

1. Start at the durable owner model, not at the largest file.
2. Trace one value flow: Placement → Offer Listing/Quote → Trade Order → Bill/Payment or Fulfillment/Provider.
3. Separate a source fact from a transport route and from a browser presentation grouping.
4. Treat every historical task claim as a hypothesis until compared with current source and durable contracts.
5. Stop exploration when the next decision is a product policy or a bounded owner slice, rather than accumulating
   another unstructured inventory.

Expected outcome: a proposed order that can be revised at named decision gates without reorganizing runtime code.
