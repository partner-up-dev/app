# Scope

## In Scope

- one collaboration object: `PR`
- home exploration, `/prd` discovery, `PR` creation, and PR detail participation
- anonymous UUID continuity, WeChat login / binding, `/me`, and `/pr/mine`
- distribution paths, WeChat share, Xiaohongshu caption / poster output, and `spm` attribution
- PR detail information architecture for meeting guidance, participant visibility, venue context, and notification-subscription management
- non-realtime PR messaging and message notifications, including participant-authored messages plus operator-authored system messages
- reliability and coordination loops: confirmation, reminders, check-in, new-partner notifications, meeting-point updates, feedback questionnaires, pairing identity, and Study Sprint
- PR-attached commerce and bounded support loops when they directly help collaboration completion
- support routing through "Need Help", author feedback, and about-page entry
- operator maintenance of PR type policy, discovery/authoring configuration, PR, and POI semantics

## Explicitly Out Of Scope

- turning the product into an independent community, content platform, or social network
- turning PR-attached commerce or support into a generic marketplace, dispatch platform, or ticketing system
- a generic discovery entrypoint detached from PR type and authoring policy
- direct publish inside Xiaohongshu
- multilingual product experience

## Deliberately Retained Current-Stage Constraints

- some real product capabilities still depend on WeChat environment differences and operator-managed configuration
